## 0.16.1 — 2026-09-26

### Fixed

- **A new service worker no longer counts as an app update when the server's newest release is the one the page already runs (a page loaded fresh during a deploy). With startAppUpdateChecks({ currentRelease }) running, the waiting worker takes over quietly: no onUpdateAvailable prompt and no auto-update reload. A genuinely newer release is still announced.** (`startAutoUpdate`)

## 0.16.0 — 2026-09-26

### Added

- **Apply app updates automatically at a safe moment with startAutoUpdate: immediately in hidden tabs, after an idle period in visible ones, never while a dialog is open, a field has focus or the app reports unsaved work**
